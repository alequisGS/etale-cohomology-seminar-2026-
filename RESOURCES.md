# Resources

## Primary

Daniel Litt — [*Étale Cohomology and the Weil Conjectures*](https://www.daniellitt.com/tale-cohomology/)

Litt's videos determine the seminar sequence. The repository is not a transcription or replacement for the course.

## Companion foundations

Jacob Tsimerman — [Étale Cohomology notes](https://www.math.utoronto.ca/~jacobt/Etale.html)

These notes are especially useful for filling prerequisite gaps and for providing a second presentation of foundational material.

## References

Litt recommends several references that may be useful as the seminar progresses:

- J. S. Milne, *Lectures on Étale Cohomology*
- J. S. Milne, *Étale Cohomology*
- Freitag-Kiehl
- Katz's lectures on the Weil conjectures

Use precise references when a contribution depends on a particular result, example, or formulation.

## Mathematical practice and AI

Terence Tao — [*Mathematics in the Age of AI*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.16753)

This essay motivates the seminar's emphasis on verification, digestion, attribution, human responsibility, and mathematical community.
