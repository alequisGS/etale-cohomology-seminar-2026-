## The full course

Litt's [course page](https://www.daniellitt.com/tale-cohomology/) lists thirty recorded lectures, each with video and notes. The pilot covers the first five. Lectures 6-30 are the map of what a continuation would look like; the topics below are Litt's own lecture summaries, abbreviated.

Topics already covered by the pilot:

| Lecture | Topics |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | Introduction, the Weil conjectures, rationality of the zeta function for curves, Serre's Kähler analogue |
| 2 | Proof of Serre's Kähler analogue, review of étale morphisms, intro to sites |
| 3 | Sites, sheaves on sites, examples of sites, the étale and fppf topologies |
| 4 | Morphisms of sites, fppf descent part I |
| 5 | fppf descent part II, beginning of the study of the category of sheaves |

The next block, if the group continues:

A natural continuation point is Lecture 6, since the pilot ends having just reached the category of sheaves. Lectures 7-10 then produce the first genuine computations, and Lecture 30 closes the arc opened in Lecture 1.
