## 90-minute architecture

```text
0-15 min    AI-free collective reconstruction
15-35 min   friction board
35-60 min   one example, theorem, or proof deeply
60-75 min   what should survive?
75-90 min   connections, questions, and community
```

### AI-free collective reconstruction

Notes closed and AI off. Collectively reconstruct:

- What problem was addressed?
- What definitions appeared?
- What were the principal statements?
- What examples mattered?

### Friction board

Discuss selected points where participants genuinely struggled. Do not try to resolve every question.

### Deep dive

Interrogate one example, non-example, theorem, or proof. Ask:

- What exactly is being claimed?
- Are the hypotheses necessary?
- Can we verify it?
- What fails if a hypothesis is removed?
- Where is the real difficulty?
- What makes this example illuminating?
- What would we want to remember?

### What should survive?

Decide what deserves to enter the group's permanent mathematical memory.

### Community

Identify connections, references, questions, possible pairs of participants, or ideas worth following.
