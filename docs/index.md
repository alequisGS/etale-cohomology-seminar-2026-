# Étale Cohomology Seminar 2026

This is a five-week pilot seminar following Daniel Litt's recorded course [*Étale Cohomology and the Weil Conjectures*](https://www.daniellitt.com/tale-cohomology/).

The purpose is not to reproduce the lectures. Litt provides the curriculum. Tools help participants prepare. The community verifies, digests, and decides what is worth remembering.

## Preparation

Each participant prepares:

```text
3 + 2 + 1 + E + R
```

- 3 things they think they understood
- 2 points of mathematical friction
- 1 question for humans
- 1 example or non-example
- 1 review of another contribution

## Five-meeting pilot

| Meeting | Litt lecture | Topics |

| [3](../meetings/03-sites-and-sheaves/) | Lecture 3 | Sites; sheaves on sites; examples of sites; étale topology; fppf topology |
| [4](../meetings/04-fppf-descent-I/) | Lecture 4 | Morphisms of sites; fppf descent, part I |
| [5](../meetings/05-fppf-descent-II/) | Lecture 5 | fppf descent, part II; beginning of the study of the category of sheaves |
