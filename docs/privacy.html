Privacy note

Registration is handled by Google Forms. Responses are stored in a private Google Sheet controlled by the organizer.

The form may collect your full name, affiliation, email, optional current stage, optional interest statement, and consent for seminar coordination.

Email is used for seminar communication. Academic stage and the interest statement help organizers understand the composition of the group.

Registration information is not automatically published. Names may later appear publicly only through ordinary GitHub contributions that participants choose to make.

Google processes Form data according to Google's own services and privacy terms.

The Google Meet link is sent by email to confirmed participants.
