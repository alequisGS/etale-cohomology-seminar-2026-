Privacy note

Registration is handled exclusively through Google Forms. Responses flow from the Google Form to a private Google Sheet, then to the seminar organizers.

The form may collect your full name, affiliation, email, GitHub username, optional current stage, optional interest statement, and consent for seminar coordination.

Email is used for seminar communication. GitHub username is used for collaboration invitations. Academic stage and the interest statement help organizers understand the composition of the group.

Registration information is not automatically published. Names may later appear publicly only through ordinary GitHub contributions that participants choose to make.

Google processes Form data according to Google's own services and privacy terms.

No alternative registration storage exists in this repository. The Google Meet link is sent by email to confirmed participants.
