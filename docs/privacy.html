Privacy note

The registration form collects your full name, affiliation, email, GitHub username, optional current stage, schedule availability when options are available, and consent for seminar coordination.

This information is used for organizing the seminar, selecting a feasible weekly meeting time, contacting participants, and inviting confirmed GitHub usernames to collaborate on the repository.

Email addresses are not published automatically. Registration responses should not be committed to the public GitHub repository.

The seminar does not operate the external form processor that may be configured for submissions. Once a provider is configured, consult that provider's own privacy terms.

Repository collaborator invitations remain a human organizer action after reviewing registrations.
